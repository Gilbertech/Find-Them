How FindMe Works

Four simple steps to connect families with the power of the community

1. Create an Account

Sign up in minutes with your name, phone number, and county to join the network.

2. Post Missing Person

Upload a clear photo and details. A small M-Pesa fee ensures all reports are genuine.

3. Community Search

Members instantly share the report across their networks and submit secure tips.

4. Verified Resolution

Police officers physically verify "found" reports before Admins give final approval.
